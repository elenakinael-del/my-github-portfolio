PUBLISHED PAPER / 00
00 — VOLATILITY FORECASTING & HAR MODELS

Options-implied volatility and the limits of directional inference.

Investigates COMEX Gold futures volatility forecasting via HAR models augmented with the CBOE Gold Volatility Index (GVZ) and jump decomposition. Evaluates seven specifications, demonstrating out-of-sample R² improvements from +10.2% up to +55.6% (Ridge regression specification), with AR-GVZ reaching marginal significance (p=0.065).

PUBLISHED PAPER / 01
01 — BEHAVIOURAL FEATURES & REGIME DETECTION

When a well-designed model still shouldn't win.

Tests whether human perception of volatility follows logarithmic Weber-Fechner scaling by building psychophysical features from CFTC positioning data, embedded in a Random Forest with 3-state HMM regime detection (Calm / Transitional / Crisis) and TreeSHAP explainability. The psychophysical features carry real, non-zero signal — but the paper's central, pre-registered result is a clean null: on the 2022–2026 structural-break test window, the simple HAR-RV benchmark (R² = 0.313) beats the full augmented model (R² = 0.125). Reporting that honestly, rather than reframing it, is the point.
