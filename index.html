I study what happens when markets stop behaving like independent agents: how macro pressure, attention, fear and herding cascade through prices and liquidity.

Mapping market state in higher dimensions.

A research engine that treats Gold futures as a non-linear system. Lorenz phase-space dynamics, volatility deformation and volume-driven particle flow combine to reveal the market regime beneath conventional charts.
